Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Scout Vision Labs ("we," "us," or "our") handles information collected through this informational website at scoutvisionlabs.site.

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, provide support, and communicate about our services. We do not sell personal information collected through this website.

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Our hosting provider and network services may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and request data, to deliver the website and maintain security. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

We do not intentionally place advertising or analytics cookies through this website. Third-party services used to deliver website resources, such as externally hosted fonts, may receive technical request information.

Data Retention and Security

We retain email correspondence only as reasonably needed to respond to requests, maintain records, or comply with applicable obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

External Links

Our website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable law.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@scoutvisionlabs.site.
